Ideas worth sitting with

Mathematics is more than formulas

A good definition opens a door. A proof shows why the door must be there.

A beautiful connection

Symmetry becomes algebra

A group captures the idea of reversible transformations that preserve structure. Instead of studying each symmetry separately, we study their composition and the laws they obey.

\( (ab)c = a(bc), \qquad e a = a e = a \)

Question to explore: Why do the symmetries of a square form a non-abelian group?

A central idea in analysis

Local information can control global behaviour

Under suitable hypotheses, a function's behaviour near every point can determine what it does across an entire domain. Complex analysis is famous for the strength of this phenomenon.

\( f \text{ holomorphic on } \mathbb C,\; f \text{ bounded} \Rightarrow f \text{ constant} \)

Question to explore: Which assumptions make Liouville's theorem possible?

Build your foundations

A path toward deeper mathematics

Advanced study becomes much more rewarding when definitions and proof techniques are strong.

STEP 01

Master the language

Sets, functions, relations, quantifiers, logic, and proof methods.

STEP 02

Build core theory

Linear algebra, real analysis, abstract algebra, and metric spaces.

STEP 03

Specialise deeply

Choose fields and study definitions, examples, counterexamples, and major theorems.

STEP 04

Read research

Read papers critically, check prerequisites, reproduce proofs, and develop precise questions.
